Why the Earth Is a Sphere, Not a Disc

That the Earth is a sphere (more precisely, a slightly flattened sphere) can be verified yourself with several simple observations - you don't have to fly into space to check.

💬 Mia asks Grandpa Theo

Mia

How do we even know this, if you can't fly into space yourself?

Grandpa Theo

Take a ship slowly disappearing over the horizon: first the hull vanishes, and the mast tip disappears last. On a flat disc, the whole ship would just keep getting smaller, not disappear piece by piece.

Mia

Are there more proofs you can see yourself?

Grandpa Theo

During a lunar eclipse, the Earth casts its shadow onto the moon - and that shadow is always round, no matter the angle. Only a sphere casts a round shadow from every direction.

More evidence

Different constellations: travel far north or south and you see different stars in the sky - on a disc, everyone everywhere would see the same sky.

Going all the way around: you can keep flying in one direction (e.g. always east) and eventually arrive back at your starting point.

Photos from space: satellites and astronauts have photographed the round Earth thousands of times.

Strictly speaking, the Earth isn't a perfect sphere - it's slightly flattened at the poles and bulges a bit at the equator, which is called an oblate spheroid. But for everyday purposes, "sphere" is a perfectly good approximation.
